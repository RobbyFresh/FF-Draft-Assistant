How “Will He Make It Back?” Works

Return Risk estimates the urgency that a tracked player will be selected before your next turn. It starts with that player's current board rank and market ADP, then scales the player-specific probability for every opponent pick using configured starter and flex openings, tier scarcity, and recent position runs. Opponent rosters advance between projected selections, so a team picking twice can fill one need before its later pick is evaluated. Positional need adjusts—but cannot create or nearly erase—the player's rank-derived probability. Open dedicated slots carry the most pressure; bench-only picks are discounted while useful starter slots remain. Superflex preserves strong QB2 demand. Without a dedicated TE slot, tight ends are treated as flex candidates and weighted primarily by projected points against RB/WR/TE alternatives; an open FLEX then carries the same slot-need weight for a qualifying TE as for an RB or WR.
